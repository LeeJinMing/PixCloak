Choose JPG, PNG or WebP files. This sample exports JPEGs, applies a size cap and longest-side limit, numbers the files, and records each result. Try the generated sample to see the full delivery format.

JPEG output has a white background. Images keep their aspect ratio and are never enlarged. Dimensions may shrink further to meet the file-size limit. Maximum input: 20 MB per image, 100 MB per batch, 30 million pixels per image.
